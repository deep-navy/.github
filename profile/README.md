# Real-world data for AI agents

**Build agents that investigate how events could affect companies and markets.**

deep.navy gives your agent access to up-to-date news, geospatial data, weather, company records and market context through MCP and APIs. Bring the evidence together in sourced research briefs that explain what happened, who may be involved and what deserves follow-up.

**[See a sourced investigation](https://deep.navy/examples/world-event-investigation)** · **[Run the examples](https://github.com/deep-navy/examples)** · **[Connect your agent](https://deep.navy/docs#client-configuration)**

## Follow the question across sources

| Your agent's question | Useful context |
| --- | --- |
| What is being reported? | News search and available article content |
| Where is it happening, and what conditions matter? | Place resolution, geospatial records and supported US weather |
| Which organizations are involved? | Company identity, SEC filings and reported facts |
| What wider context could change the interpretation? | Energy observations, futures positioning and economic indicators |

Use the sources your question needs alongside your existing tools. Your agent establishes the relationships, weighs the evidence and writes the assessment. The examples preserve source dates and distinguish supported findings from open questions.

## Start with one real research question

1. [Sign in and create a tool key](https://deep.navy/signin).
2. Connect a remote MCP client to **https://mcp.deep.navy/mcp** using the [client instructions](https://deep.navy/docs#client-configuration).
3. Run the [event investigation starter](https://github.com/deep-navy/examples), then adapt it to your company, place or industry question.

The service is **free during preview**. No card required. [Usage limits apply](https://deep.navy/docs/limits).
